Visual System · Design Catalog

PROJECT ZARATHUSTRA

The reference sheet for the look: palette, type, the signature motif, components, and the game/media touchstones — tuned to read as a serious quant red-team tool to a fintech room, not a crypto LARP. Pick a theme direction, then everything below is the recommended (Terminal Noir) system.
00

North Star

one sentence the whole system serves
A quant red-team terminal that happens to be playing a bullet-hell game. Bloomberg-black density and mono numerals buy the credibility; the attack swarm buys the wow; the Monad purple laser and Rain magenta headline give each judge their brand doing a job. The occult is a whisper — one oracle line, one wax seal — never the costume.

Theme Directions

three moods to choose from — same bones, different intensity
Terminal Noir recommended
Near-black indigo, restrained glow, mono-first. Reads as a risk desk that a bullet-swarm happens to run through.
#0E0B1A#836EF9#F0197A#7CF2B0#F5B849
Use if: you want maximum credibility with a crypto-native but money-serious room. The safe-but-striking default.
Neon Sacrament high-risk
Deeper void, hotter neon, more bloom and glow. Turns the oracle/Avesta flavor up toward ritual. More memorable, more polarizing.
#0A0710#9B7BFF#FF2D8B#63F5A9#FFC94A
Use if: you want the demo to be the one people photograph — and you trust the numbers to keep it from reading "degen."
Clean Ledger conservative
Slate-black, desaturated indigo/rose/teal. Looks like a real institutional risk dashboard. Drops nearly all the myth.
#0C0E14#6C63C4#D65A86#57C7A8#E0B15A
Use if: a judge or partner is old-guard fintech. Probably overkill here — Rain and Monad are crypto-native — but it's your escape hatch.

Reference Board — Games & Media

what to borrow, and exactly how much
Touhou / Ikaruga danmaku
Borrow: the bullet-swarm as the hero motif — dense, patterned, one safe path through a wall of hazard. It's the whole visual thesis, double-coded as attack-surface viz.
Dose: HIGH. This is the signature. Just never label it "danmaku" on screen — let it be recognized, not announced.
SETTLE $49.97 L5 ALLOW GATE cap $50 allowlist ✓ SLIP $0.03 sealed / round RANK #5 succ → #1 $-risk HEAD e5acaa05382d VERIFIED
Bloomberg / trading terminal density
Borrow: mono numerals, dense readouts, dark chrome, "every pixel is data." This is what buys you credibility in a fintech room.
Dose: HIGH. The base register. If in doubt, make it look more like a terminal and less like a landing page.
Homeworld / EVE tactical HUD structure
Borrow: thin reticle lines, range rings, the allowlist "ring" the bounce-dots deflect off. Gives the stage a sense of a defended perimeter.
Dose: MEDIUM. Structural hints only — grid, ring, laser. Don't turn it into a space game.
Persona 5 kinetic type energy
Borrow: one angled, oversized, high-contrast moment — the "ROB IT" button, the $-headline pop. A single hit of attitude.
Dose: LOW. Exactly one kinetic beat. More than that and it stops reading as finance.

Borrow: the wax-seal "chain verified" stamp and the "Thus Spoke the Ledger" receipt header. The memorable hook in a room of forgettable "AI agent security" pitches.
Dose: TRACE. Two touches only: the receipt header + the seal. No fire, temples, or runes as decoration.

09

Do / Don't

the credibility guardrails

Do

  • Lead every surface with a mono dollar number.
  • Keep both brand colors load-bearing — purple = the laser, magenta = the headline.
  • Let amber appear once at a time; it's the eye-magnet.
  • Show the gate working (splats, bounces) before you show it failing.
  • Keep the myth to exactly two touches: the header and the seal.

Don't

  • No fire, temples, runes, or skulls as decoration — reads LARP.
  • No serif or "ancient" display fonts. The naming carries the myth.
  • No rainbow dashboards — this palette is disciplined for a reason.
  • Don't autoplay-then-fail; gate motion behind a click.
  • Don't say "on-chain" or "seeded" unless it truly is — the room will check.
